## Notes

- 2026-10-15: implemented in `etl/preprocess_htot.py`. Silver (`data/silver/building_<id>.parquet`) keeps all hours on a full hourly grid with only the sentinel and negative-solar fixes. Gold (`data/gold/htot_oslo.parquet`) applies the gap rules above and adds a `split` column.
- 2026-10-15: gold also has calendar features `hour` (0-23), `weekday` (Monday = 0) and `month` (1-12), plain integers in the data's fixed UTC+1 time.
- 2026-10-17: `SolGlob == 0` rows are kept. Zero is a real value (no sun) in about 28% of rows, mostly evening, night and early morning. Dropping them would remove those hours and break the hourly windows.
- 2026-10-17: modelling plan. Horizons 1 h, 6 h and 24 h. Inputs: past `HtTot`, weather, calendar, `year_of_construction`. Future weather comes from measured values (a "perfect forecast" replay), so setups can be compared on equal terms. Real forecast errors are not included, so scores are an upper bound for deployment.
- 2026-10-17: validation uses a rolling-origin (expanding window) split, never random. Features are scaled with statistics fitted on the training part only. Metrics: MAE and RMSE in kWh (`HtTot` is Wh per hour, divide by 1000) and WAPE (sum of absolute errors / sum of actuals), compared with a seasonal-naive baseline.
- 2026-10-17: the 70/15/15 split in the table above is replaced. Final test block = last ~15% of the time span, 2022-03-01 to 2023-10-22, untouched. Before it: 10 rolling-origin folds of 6 months each (validation 2017-03-01 to 2022-03-01), expanding training window, same calendar dates for all buildings. A window belongs to the split of its target hour. Look-back 168 h. A window is valid only if all hours from t-167 to t+horizon exist. Implemented in `etl/windows.py`, tested in `tests/test_windows.py`. The gold `split` column was removed. Not using `year_of_construction` as input (same value for every row of a building, only 3 buildings).
- 2026-10-17: valid windows are much fewer than gold rows (e.g. 8105: 35,244 of 76,546 for horizon 1) because dropped zero and flat hours break the 169-hour runs. Summer folds suffer most (about 1,200 validation windows against 4,380 hours in a 6-month block).
- 2026-10-17: `HtTot == 0` rows are now kept in June, July and August (heating off is real information) and still dropped in other months (`SUMMER_MONTHS` in `etl/preprocess_htot.py`; set it to `()` to drop zeros all year). Gold rows: 8105 90,863, 8107 88,009, 8116 90,938. Valid windows for horizon 1 rose to 51,624 / 34,900 / 57,091. Building 8107 has one summer run of 1,238 zero hours (about 52 days), (2014-06-24 to 2014-08-14). Checked: it matches the school summer break, `ElImp` stays normal (about 75 kWh per hour), `Tout` is 18 to 25 C and 8105 is also mostly zero then. Most likely real, so the zeros are kept. Open: the zero share in June to August is 64 to 66% in 2013 and 2014 but 12 to 34% in later years, so early summers differ (control change or meter change?). Treat 2013 and 2014 summers with care when reading fold scores.
- 2026-10-17: gold has `WindDir_sin` and `WindDir_cos` instead of `WindDir` (silver keeps the angle). They are computed before the short-gap interpolation, so a gap across north (359 to 1 degrees) is filled correctly. They are not scaled.
- 2026-10-17: baselines and metrics in `modeling/baselines.py` and `modeling/metrics.py`. Baseline = seasonal naive, lag 24 h or 168 h, whichever has the lower WAPE on the fold's training windows (persistence is available for horizon 1). Scores: MAE and RMSE in kWh, WAPE and skill (`1 - WAPE_model / WAPE_baseline`), per fold, building, horizon and season. Summer is reported as MAE and RMSE in kWh only (WAPE and skill left empty). A baseline value always exists for a valid window because the copied hour lies inside the 168 h history.
- 2026-10-17: reference result, seasonal naive on all 10 validation folds, three buildings pooled: WAPE 0.236 (horizon 1), 0.235 (6), 0.235 (24); winter MAE about 47 kWh, summer MAE about 6 kWh. Lag 24 was chosen in every fold. A model has to beat these numbers.
- 2026-10-17: the number of `HtTot` files is 37 (33 Oslo + 4 Drammen), none empty. The earlier count of 38 was wrong.
- Dropping `HtTot == 0` also removes legitimate summer hours when heating is off. If the model later fails in summer, revisit this rule first.- 2026-10-17: first LightGBM run (`modeling/gbm.py`, `python -m modeling.run_gbm`, output in `results/gbm/`, about 39 min). No `building_id`, no `year_of_construction`, fixed settings (400 trees, L1 loss), no tuning. Pooled (all buildings in training), all-season WAPE, residual target: 0.101 / 0.139 / 0.139 for horizon 1 / 6 / 24 against baseline 0.236 / 0.235 / 0.235 (skill 0.57 / 0.41 / 0.41). Direct target is similar (0.098 / 0.140 / 0.144). Leave-one-building-out (unseen building), residual: WAPE 0.118 / 0.174 / 0.172 (skill 0.50 / 0.26 / 0.27). Weakest on 8107 at horizons 6 and 24 (skill 0.15 / 0.18). Persistence has not yet been compared at horizon 1.
- 2026-10-17: persistence at horizon 1 (`modeling/persistence_report.py`, output `results/gbm/horizon1_vs_persistence*.csv`, no retraining). Persistence WAPE 0.118 against seasonal naive 0.236, so it is the stronger baseline at 1 h. Trees trained on all buildings: WAPE 0.098 (direct) / 0.101 (residual), skill versus persistence 0.17 / 0.15. Trees on an unseen building: WAPE 0.115 / 0.118, skill versus persistence 0.03 / 0.005, so no real gain over persistence at 1 h. The residual anchor and `y_base` in `modeling/gbm.py` still use the seasonal naive (not changed).
- 2026-10-17: tree tuning implemented, not yet run (`modeling/tuning.py`, tests in `tests/test_tuning.py`). Random search, 30 trials plus trial 0 = current default settings. Tuning folds 03, 04, 07, 08; the other six folds are check folds; the test block is never loaded. Score = mean WAPE over {pooled, leave-one-building-out} x {horizon 6, 24}, residual target, one shared setting for all horizons. Searched: objective (l1, huber, l2), `num_leaves` 15-127, `min_child_samples` 20-400, `subsample` 0.5-1, `colsample_bytree` 0.4-1, `reg_lambda` 0.01-30, huber `alpha` 2-30 kWh. Number of trees by early stopping on the last 60 days of the training period (metric l1, patience 50, max 1500), then a refit on all training windows with that many trees. `confirm` reruns the best setting on all 10 folds (both targets, horizons 1, 6, 24) and writes a comparison with the first run. Trials are appended to `results/tuning/trials.jsonl` and a stopped search resumes.
- 2026-10-18: tree tuning (`python -m modeling.tuning search` then `confirm`, output `results/tuning/`). 31 trials on folds 03, 04, 07, 08 (horizons 6 and 24, pooled and leave-one-building-out, residual target). Default settings scored WAPE 0.1599, best trial (26: L1, 90 leaves, min child 197, subsample 0.75, colsample 0.94, lambda 4.9) 0.1587, a 0.75% relative gain. L2 trials about 0.19, Huber worse and slower. Confirmation on all 10 folds: WAPE change versus the first run between -1% and +1% on the six check folds at horizons 6 and 24 (and on the tuning folds), and slightly worse at horizon 1 for an unseen building (+1.6% check folds, +4.3% tuning folds; horizon 1 was not tuned). Conclusion: the first settings were already on a plateau, tuning gives no real gain. Decision: keep the original settings unless a later step says otherwise.
